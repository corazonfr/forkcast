Forkcast

Ends the "where should we eat?" argument. Then hands you off to DoorDash or Uber Eats.

1

Who's eating?

Add what each person usually likes and what they won't eat. Food types, cuisines, or specific places all work. A like moves a place up the list. One nope rules it out.

2

Where are you?

or

Forkcast doesn't deliver anything. It picks the place, then sends you to that restaurant on DoorDash or Uber Eats.
